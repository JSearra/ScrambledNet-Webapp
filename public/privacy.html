Scrambled Net – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

This policy applies to the Scrambled Net game on the web, on Android and on Windows (Microsoft Store).

Data we collect

None. Scrambled Net does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

Data stored on your device

The game saves your preferences (sound on/off and the selected theme) in your device's local storage so they are remembered between sessions. This data never leaves your device, and you can remove it at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the game.

Internet access

The game's files are downloaded from its website and cached so it can be played offline. Loading those files is the only network activity; no information about you or your games is sent.

Children

Because no personal information is collected, the game is suitable for players of all ages.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions can be raised on the project's issue tracker: github.com/JSearra/ScrambledNet-Webapp/issues.
